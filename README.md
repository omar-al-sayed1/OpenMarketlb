# OpenMarketlb

OpenMarketlb is an online marketplace for Lebanon that connects local shops with customers. It brings many independent sellers into one place where people can discover products, order them, and have them delivered — while shops get the tools to run and grow their business.

Customers can browse and search across shops, filter by category, save items, and check out by card, wallet, or cash on delivery. They can verify their phone, sign in with Google, earn loyalty points, message shops directly, find nearby stores on a map, track their orders, and get help from an AI shopping assistant.

Shops get their own storefront to manage products, orders, promotions, a wallet, and analytics, while admins oversee the whole platform — shops, orders, users, categories, payments, and branding — from a single dashboard. The experience is available on the web and as a mobile app, and the brand's look (including its colors) is controlled by the admin and applied everywhere.

## Features

- Product browsing, search, categories, filters, wishlist, cart & checkout
- Wallet payment and cash on delivery
- Phone (SMS OTP) and Google sign-in, loyalty points, and in-app messaging
- Shops map with locations, live order tracking, and an AI shopping assistant
- Seller storefronts with products, orders, wallet, and analytics
- Admin dashboard for shops, orders, users, categories, and settings
- Available on web and mobile, with admin-controlled branding

## Notes

- Brand colors are set by the admin in Settings and applied across web and mobile.
- Legal pages: `/privacy` and `/delete-account`.
